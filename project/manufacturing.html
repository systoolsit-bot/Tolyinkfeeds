Production stages

Eight stages behind every bag

STAGE 01

Raw material sourcing

We buy grains, oilseed meals, protein sources and minerals from suppliers whose materials can be checked against our specifications. Purchasing decisions consider quality, consistency and delivery reliability, not price alone.

STAGE 02

Ingredient selection

Each incoming delivery is inspected and sampled before it is accepted into storage. Ingredients that do not meet the agreed specification are rejected and recorded, so they never reach a production run.

STAGE 03

Feed formulation

Rations are calculated for the species and production stage they are designed for, such as broiler starter, layer or pig grower. Formulations are reviewed before each production run, and changes to ingredients are checked against the nutritional targets.

STAGE 04

Grinding and mixing

Ingredients are ground to the particle size required for the feed type and then blended in batches. Thorough mixing gives each bag a uniform nutritional profile, so every animal receives the same balance of nutrients.

STAGE 05

Production and processing

Depending on the product, the mix is processed into mash or pellets, then cooled and screened. Each step is controlled to keep the feed stable in storage and easy for animals to eat.

STAGE 06

Quality control

Samples are taken during processing and before dispatch. Each batch is recorded with its ingredients, formulation and results, so that any product can be traced back to the production run it came from.

STAGE 07

Packaging

Feed is weighed and sealed in bags that protect it from moisture and contamination. Each bag is labelled with the product name, batch details and handling information.

STAGE 08

Distribution

Finished stock is stored in dry conditions and dispatched to farms, feed stores, distributors and bulk buyers. Our sales team confirms delivery timelines and quantities when you place your order.
