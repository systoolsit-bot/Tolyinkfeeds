Veterinary products

Veterinary drugs and animal healthcare products

We supply veterinary drugs, vaccines and animal healthcare products for poultry and livestock businesses. Every product is intended for use under the guidance of a qualified veterinary professional.

Use under professional guidance. Veterinary medicines are not a substitute for professional diagnosis. Use them only as directed by a qualified veterinary professional, follow the product label and withdrawal periods, store products as instructed and keep records of treatments.

Biosecurity

Disinfectants and animal healthcare products

Good hygiene protects animals and reduces the spread of disease on farm. We supply disinfectants and biosecurity products for cleaning houses, equipment and footbaths, along with routine animal healthcare items.

Disinfectants must be used at the concentration and contact time stated on the label.
