Our manufacturing philosophy
Manufacturing philosophy

Controlled processes, honest formulations

We formulate each feed for the species and production stage it is intended for. We do not add claims to a product that the formulation cannot support, and we make sure the process that produced a batch can be traced.

Our production teams work to documented procedures, so the feed produced this week matches the feed produced last month.

Quality assurance

Our quality assurance commitment

Quality checks run through every stage of production, not only at the end of the line.

  • Raw materials are checked against specification on arrival
  • Formulations are reviewed before each production run
  • Samples are taken during processing and before dispatch
  • Batch records are kept so that any product can be traced
