Feed manufacturing

From raw material to packaged feed

Every batch follows a controlled route from ingredient intake to dispatch, so the feed you order is the feed you receive.

  1. 01

    Raw material selection

    Grains, protein meals and minerals are selected against specification before they enter the mill.

  2. 02

    Ingredient formulation

    Rations are formulated for each species and production stage.

  3. 03

    Grinding and mixing

    Ingredients are milled to the correct particle size and blended for uniform nutrition.

  4. 04

    Feed processing

    Mash or pellet production, followed by cooling and quality checks.

  5. 05

    Packaging and distribution

    Feeds are bagged, labelled and dispatched to farms, stores and distributors.
